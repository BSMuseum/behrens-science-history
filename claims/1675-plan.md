## Rule: one text, one slot (proposed clause 13; Bruce, 2026-11-22)

Prompted by Rohault's gaps c. 1675. Under clause 5 the next French course in the count was Goudin, who already fills the Catholic German slot. Bruce: "duplicating him just gives him two votes."

13. A text fills one region's slot in a snapshot, never two. Where a region's text has no section on a topic, clause 5 passes the topic to the next course in that region's count that fills no other slot and meets clause 7's minimum (two editions printed in the region in the window). If no course qualifies, the region's cell is left blank for that topic, and the blank is recorded as a finding.

Applied to 1675 (counts from the USTC, 2026-11-16):

- **France**, where Rohault has no section: the next courses are Goudin (3, taken), Gautruche (1) and Du Hamel (1). None qualifies, so France is blank on those topics. The blanks are themselves the finding: Rohault's Cartesian physics stops at the human body.
- **Catholic German lands**, where Goudin has no section (the magnet): the next courses are Heinlein (1) and Gautruche (1), so the cell is blank.
- **Protestant German lands and British Isles:** apply the same way if gaps appear. For Britain, Sennert's *Epitome* (3) is next in line.

  - Goudin II q. 3, the elements, pp. 122-132 (t. 3 canvases 146-156), and III q. 3 a. 3 § 4, meteors in water and earth and the origin of springs and rivers, pp. 274-285 (canvases 300-311). The OCR search found no tides passage; check.
  - Le Grand VI a. 7 springs (from p. 340, leaf 399), a. 11 water and a. 12 tides (about pp. 359-366, leaves 418-425)

Measures:

- **Claude usage:** Bruce notes the usage bar before and after each tab. Each tab also reports its tool calls and the number of page images it viewed.
- **Quality:** both tabs check every quotation against the page image before publishing, and report what the check found.
- **OpenRouter tab:** also reports requests per model, failures, and where the two text models disagreed.
